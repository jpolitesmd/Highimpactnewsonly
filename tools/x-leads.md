- **Russian ballistic missiles hit Kyiv overnight; 21-year-old woman killed, at least 8 injured** — Russia fired several waves of ballistic missiles at Kyiv overnight into Monday (Oct. 19), around 11 p.m., 3 a.m. and 7 a.m. local time. Mayor Vitali Klitschko said a 21-year-old woman was killed; at least eight people were injured, a 25-story apartment building and a medical center in Solomianskyi district were damaged, and the above-ground Chernihivska and Lisova metro stations closed for repairs. In nearby Irpin, the Kyiv regional military administration head said six were injured, including three children, and three were missing. Ukrainian outlets report the sirens sounded only as or after the missiles hit, a day after Zelenskyy said U.S. intelligence sharing had resumed; attribute that to Ukrainian officials and reporters. - **Indonesian stocks crash to a six-year low after MSCI threatens to drop the market from emerging-market indexes** — The Jakarta Composite fell more than 5% Monday (Oct. 19) to its lowest since October 2020, after an 11.6% plunge Friday, its worst day in nearly three decades. MSCI said Friday it is considering moving Indonesia from "emerging market" straight to "standalone" status over opaque ownership and trading, which would cut it out of the indexes global funds track; a final decision is expected by June. The exchange cut its lower auto-rejection limit to 7% from 15%; the index is down about 40% this year and the rupiah weakened to about 18,040 per dollar. Goldman Sachs estimates a full removal could trigger about $5.6 billion in passive outflows (attribute). Source: Reuters, https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/panic-selling-hits-indonesian-markets-on-msci-expulsion-threat-4956386 (Reuters via Bangkok Post: https://www.bangkokpost.com/business/general/3339705/indonesian-stocks-slide-to-sixyear-low-as-msci-warning-weighs; CNBC: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/19/indonesian-stocks-msci-downgrade-fears-mount.html). - **Mexican man dies in ICE custody at Laredo detention center, the seventh ICE detention death in Texas this year** — Emiliano Andrade-Covarrubias, 56, was found unresponsive in his cell during a security check about 10 a.m. Saturday (Oct. 17) at the GEO Group-run Rio Grande Processing Center in Laredo; staff performed CPR and called emergency services.
