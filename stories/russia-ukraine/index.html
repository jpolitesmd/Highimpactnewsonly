Russia's war in Ukraine

Russian strikes on Ukraine, Ukraine's response and U.S. aid.

    A broader session with national-security advisers from the U.K., France and Germany plus NATO, the EU and U.S. European Command is set for 11:30 a.m. ET. Overnight, Russia launched 136 drones at Ukraine, killing one person in Zaporizhzhia, while Russia said it downed 505 Ukrainian drones over 19 regions.

  2. Impact 4

    Russia's overnight attack on Ukraine killed at least 30 people, up from 11 this morning, including 19 in a Pryluky apartment building.

    A Russian cruise missile hit a residential building in Pryluky, Chernihiv region, killing 19 people, five of them children, and injuring 55, Ukraine's emergency service said as rescuers kept searching the rubble. Authorities count at least 30 dead and 138 injured across Ukraine over the past day, including three in Kyiv and two each in Kremenchuk, Kirovohrad and Sumy regions. Ukraine's Air Force said Russia fired ballistic missiles, 48 cruise missiles (39 stopped) and 130 drones (117 stopped).

    Why impact 4: The toll nearly tripled during the day, making it the deadliest Russian strike on Ukraine in about a month.

  3. Impact 4

    Russia's overnight missile and drone attack on Ukraine killed at least 11 people, including five in a Pryluky apartment building, Ukraine's president said.

    President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said Russia fired about 70 missiles, many of them ballistic, plus attack drones at Kyiv and at least 12 regions, with energy infrastructure a key target. A missile hit a five-story apartment building in Pryluky, in the Chernihiv region, killing five and injuring 26, including five children. Two people were killed in Kyiv and two in Oleksandriia, and apartment buildings were damaged in Dnipro and Kremenchuk. The attack came on Vladimir Putin's 74th birthday. Separately, Russian officials said they intercepted almost 900 Ukrainian drones overnight.

    Why impact 4: It is one of the deadliest Russian barrages in weeks and again targets Ukraine's power grid ahead of winter.

    WorldABC News (Reuters) ↗
  4. Impact 4

    Drones sank one merchant ship and set another on fire inside Bulgaria's Black Sea economic zone, Bulgaria said; the sunken ship's crew is missing.

    Sea and air drones hit the two ships about 70 nautical miles east of Byala early Tuesday local time. The Togo-flagged, Turkish-owned Alfa Watan sank and its crew is unaccounted for. The Palau-flagged Able, carrying wheat, caught fire; all 18 crew were rescued and taken to Varna, and one is in critical condition with severe burns. Prime Minister Rumen Radev called the attack "absolutely unacceptable" and said the drones were "clearly either Russian or Ukrainian"; who launched them has not been established. Bulgaria is in contact with NATO, and President Iliana Iotova called a national security council meeting for Wednesday.

    Why impact 4: A ship was sunk inside a NATO member's waters, spreading the war's shipping risk to the western Black Sea and the grain trade.

    WorldBTA (Bulgarian News Agency) ↗
  5. Impact 4

    Ukraine launched about 650 drones at Moscow overnight, killing two people in one of the largest attacks on the Russian capital in two years, Russian officials said.

    Moscow Mayor Sergei Sobyanin said most of the drones were shot down, 105 of them close to the city. Moscow region Gov. Andrei Vorobyov said one person died in Radumlya and one in Barybino, and a drone set a retail warehouse on fire in Litvinovo. Reports put the injured at six to nine. The 650 figure comes from Russian officials; Ukraine has not confirmed it. Russia's Defense Ministry said it struck Ukraine's Black Sea ports of Chornomorsk and Izmail overnight.

    Why impact 4: It is a major escalation of Ukraine's long-range strikes on Russia's capital.

    WorldFrance 24 (AFP, Reuters, AP) ↗
  6. Impact 3

    Russia launched 205 drones at Ukraine overnight; jet-powered drones killed one person and injured eight in Kharkiv, officials said.

Kharkiv Mayor Ihor Terekhov said three Banderol jet drones struck near homes in the Industrialnyi district. Separately, President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said Sunday the U.S. has proposed technical-level three-way talks with Russia by the end of October and that Ukraine responded positively.

    Why impact 3: Russia is following through on its vow to widen strikes as Washington pushes for new talks.

  7. Impact 3

    Russia's Defense Ministry said it will intensify strikes on Kyiv and other Ukrainian regions, as drones hit a Kyiv bridge for a fourth straight day.

    The ministry said the stepped-up attacks on military, defense-industry and related infrastructure respond to President Volodymyr Zelenskiy's vow in a Reuters interview to hit more Russian oil refineries. Kyiv Mayor Vitali Klitschko said two people were hurt Sunday when a drone struck the Northern Bridge's roadway, a second straight day of hits there. Zelenskiy said a drone killed one person at a Sumy medical-supplies warehouse and six were hurt at a Dnipro communications site; he said Russia fired more than 1,900 drones, 1,700 glide bombs and 25 missiles over the past week. German Chancellor Friedrich Merz made an unannounced visit to Kyiv.

    Why impact 3: Moscow is openly targeting the capital's river crossings and says the campaign will widen.

    Sandu said Saturday on X that she condemned the morning's attack on Ukraine and that five weapons, including Banderol missiles and drones, violated Moldovan airspace and exploded on its soil. Ukraine's air force said Moscow launched Banderol cruise missiles overnight at the Odesa region, which borders Moldova. In Kyiv, Mayor Vitalii Klitschko said a Russian strike damaged the Northern Bridge's road surface and trolleybus cables and suspended traffic; the Associated Press reported no casualties. Russia's Defense Ministry said it hit the bridge, claiming it is used to move troops and military cargo. Friday's attacks had already forced full or partial closures of three of Kyiv's six road bridges over the Dnipro.

    Why impact 3: Munitions from the overnight barrage detonated on the ground in Moldova, and a second major Kyiv bridge was hit.

  10. Impact 3

    Russia's heaviest energy-grid assault in months killed seven people and forced emergency power cuts in Kyiv.

    Ukrenergo imposed emergency cuts in Kyiv and three nearby regions — the first such capital cuts since spring — after strikes hit power plants and other energy sites. President Zelensky said a child was among the seven dead and critical infrastructure was hit in five regions. Ukraine's air force said defenses intercepted 254 of 285 drones by early evening, many of them faster jet-powered models, plus a ballistic missile. Russia said it struck power plants near Kyiv, an industrial complex in Odesa region and port infrastructure at Ismail.

    Why impact 3: Marks the opening of Russia's expected winter campaign against Ukraine's power and heat.
