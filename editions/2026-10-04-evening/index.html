  1. Impact 3

    France's high-school protests will close up to 500 schools fully or partly Monday, the education minister said; 24 have been burned or ransacked.

    Education Minister Edouard Geffray said Sunday that lessons will be "totally or partly suspended" at 400 to 500 high schools where security conditions are not met, about the same number as Friday, with a "partial, progressive return" to classes. He said 24 schools have been "burned or ransacked" and 78 education staff injured since the movement began. More than 5,000 people, about 85 percent of them minors, have been arrested. Prime Minister Sebastien Lecornu sent ministers a roadmap Sunday asking that classes resume wherever safety allows and setting a timetable to answer some student demands, including school renovation talks with regional presidents and Parcoursup proposals by the end of October. In the Paris region, more than 160 of 468 public high schools will be closed Monday, regional president Valerie Pecresse said. A 16-year-old was detained Sunday over Friday's assault on a 59-year-old teacher who was doused with gasoline and struck in La Ciotat. Organizers have called for more protests Tuesday.

    Why impact 3: The protests have now closed hundreds of schools for a second week and drawn a direct order from the prime minister.

  2. Impact 3

    Russia's Defense Ministry said it will intensify strikes on Kyiv and other Ukrainian regions, as drones hit a Kyiv bridge for a fourth straight day.

    The ministry said the stepped-up attacks on military, defense-industry and related infrastructure respond to President Volodymyr Zelenskiy's vow in a Reuters interview to hit more Russian oil refineries. Kyiv Mayor Vitali Klitschko said two people were hurt Sunday when a drone struck the Northern Bridge's roadway, a second straight day of hits there. Zelenskiy said a drone killed one person at a Sumy medical-supplies warehouse and six were hurt at a Dnipro communications site; he said Russia fired more than 1,900 drones, 1,700 glide bombs and 25 missiles over the past week. German Chancellor Friedrich Merz made an unannounced visit to Kyiv.

    Why impact 3: Moscow is openly targeting the capital's river crossings and says the campaign will widen.

    WorldReuters ↗
  3. Impact 3

    Ethiopian government-allied forces entered Mekelle, the Tigray capital, after TPLF rebels announced they had withdrawn following renewed fighting.

    The Tigray People's Liberation Front said Saturday its forces had pulled out of Mekelle, which they seized along with its airport last month; a witness said government-allied fighters entered the city Sunday. Residents described looting of the TPLF administration office and regional police headquarters as rebel fighters left. Internet and phone outages have made events hard to verify. Ethiopia closed its embassy in Eritrea on Thursday and expelled 10 Eritrean diplomats; Eritrea said it was cutting diplomatic ties.

    Why impact 3: Control of Tigray's capital has changed hands as fighting returns to a region where a 2020–2022 war killed hundreds of thousands.

    WorldAssociated Press ↗
  4. Impact 2

    The air ambulance search moves to the seabed: the Coast Guard says sonar-equipped craft will scan near the debris field about 20 miles off Nantucket.

    At a Sunday briefing at Base Boston, the Coast Guard said crews with the New York Air National Guard and Massachusetts authorities have searched about 2,840 square miles for the six people aboard the Gulfstream G100 medical flight from Bermuda to Boston, and are working with Massachusetts authorities to get sonar-equipped vessels over the seafloor. Next of kin are being kept informed. The six — four Canadians and two Bermudians — remain missing.

    Why impact 2: Moving to a seabed search signals the effort is shifting toward locating the wreckage.

    U.S.Associated Press ↗
  5. Impact 2

    Trump announced a federal AI task force, the "Super Intelligence Force," led by Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton.

    In a Truth Social post Sunday, the president said the force will coordinate federal engagement with AI companies, consumers, public-interest and religious groups, and infrastructure providers. FTC Chairman Andrew Ferguson, Pentagon technology chief Emil Michael and OPM Director Scott Kupor will also lead it, reporting to Trump and Chief of Staff Susie Wiles. It follows last week's White House meeting where OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, Nvidia and SpaceXAI signed voluntary safety standards, including internal evaluations, outside audits and board reviews. Trump also named John Coale special presidential envoy for hostage affairs.

    Why impact 2: It names who in government will oversee the biggest AI companies after their voluntary safety pledge.

    Tech & SpaceCBS News ↗
  6. Impact 2

    A shooting at a block party in Vienna, Georgia, killed two people and injured 35, the Georgia Bureau of Investigation said.

    The GBI said Dooly County deputies were on patrol at the neighborhood party when shots were fired from the 900 block of Pine Street at about 1:25 a.m. Sunday. Two people were found dead in the 600 block of Pine Street, and victims were taken to several regional hospitals. No arrest has been announced; a $5,000 reward has been offered for information.

    Why impact 2: It is one of the largest mass shootings by number wounded this year.
