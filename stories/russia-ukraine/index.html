  8. Impact 4

    The new Russia sanctions law's tariffs and most of its sanctions were waived by President Trump on Friday, days before 100% duties on big Russian-oil buyers were due.

    A Friday executive order implementing the Lindsey O. Graham Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act of 2026, which Trump signed Sept. 18, waives the law's 500% duty on Russian goods, its 100% duties on the five largest buyers of Russian oil and gas making purchases on or after Oct. 18, and its duties on the top five countries helping Russia evade sanctions, along with most of its sanctions sections. Trump sent Congress a certification that the waiver is in the national interest; it lasts until the President or the Secretary of State decides further action is appropriate. Russia sanctions under earlier executive orders stay in place, and the U.S. Trade Representative will keep monitoring buyers of Russian energy.

    Why impact 4: Tariffs aimed at major buyers of Russian oil such as China and India will not start Sunday, while oil prices are already high.
