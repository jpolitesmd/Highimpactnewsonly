The Cornell fraternity assault case

Criminal and campus reviews of a 2024 Cornell fraternity sexual-assault case after a civil lawsuit and the local prosecutor’s earlier decision not to charge.

5 updates · newest first

  1. Impact 3

    One of the men accused in the Cornell assault case asked a New York court to remove Attorney General Letitia James as special prosecutor.

    Gillio Lopes, one of seven men named in Jane Doe's civil lawsuit, filed an Article 78 petition in Albany County Supreme Court on Oct. 7 arguing that Gov. Kathy Hochul's appointment of James is improper. The filing points to James's Sept. 28 social media post supporting Jane Doe, three days before her appointment, as evidence of bias, and asks the court to void the appointment and name a different prosecutor. A judge's order schedules oral arguments for Oct. 29. James remains special prosecutor, no criminal charges have been filed, and the men deny wrongdoing.

    Why impact 3: A court will now weigh whether the state's lead prosecutor can stay on the reopened criminal investigation.

    U.S.ABC News ↗
  2. Impact 3

    New York Attorney General Letitia James's office said the Tompkins County DA agreed to cooperate with its Cornell criminal investigation.

    A spokesperson for James said Tuesday that the investigation is led by the office's Criminal Justice Division and began Thursday evening after the governor's appointment. Since then the office has spoken with District Attorney Matthew Van Houten, Jane Doe's attorney, and several candidates for outside counsel to support the investigation. Van Houten has said his office will cooperate fully. No criminal charges have been filed, and the seven men named in the civil lawsuit deny wrongdoing.

    Why impact 3: The state's criminal inquiry is staffing up with the local prosecutor's cooperation, separate from Cornell's own review.

    U.S.CNN ↗
  3. Impact 3

    Cornell's trustees hired former Deputy U.S. Attorney General Sally Yates to lead an independent review of the 2024 Chi Phi assault case.

    A new Special Committee of Cornell's Board of Trustees said Tuesday that Yates, a partner at King & Spalding, will review how the university prevents and responds to sexual assault and misconduct, including campus police, hearings and discipline, coordination with prosecutors, and oversight of fraternities and sororities. Her team will also examine Cornell's response to the October 2024 allegations by Jane Doe against Chi Phi members, including whether it followed its own policies. Yates will report to the committee, and the board said it intends to share the findings with the Cornell community. The state attorney general's separate criminal investigation continues; no criminal charges have been filed.

    Why impact 3: A former top Justice Department official now leads the outside probe of Cornell's handling of the case, alongside the state's criminal inquiry.

    U.S.Cornell University ↗
  4. Impact 3

    Gov. Kathy Hochul appointed Attorney General Letitia James as special prosecutor in the Cornell fraternity sexual-assault case.

    Executive Order 64, signed Oct. 1, requires the attorney general to investigate the Oct. 19–20, 2024 allegations in Jane Doe v. Cornell University and, if warranted, present evidence to a grand jury and prosecute. The order displaces and supersedes the Tompkins County district attorney’s jurisdiction on the matter. Hochul said newly released information undercut her faith in the local DA’s ability to handle the case. On Friday James said her office had opened an active criminal investigation. Cornell said it supports the appointment. No criminal charges have been filed.

    Why impact 3: The state took the case from the local prosecutor after a 2024 no-charge decision and a civil lawsuit drew statewide backlash.

    U.S.New York Governor ↗
  5. Impact 3

    Cornell agreed to an independent review of its handling of a 2024 fraternity sexual-assault case as prosecutors reopen a charging look.

    After a September civil lawsuit and public backlash, Tompkins County District Attorney Matthew Van Houten said his office is reexamining the case it declined to charge in 2024. On Thursday the accuser's attorney told NPR she told Cornell police in 2024 she was raped — contradicting the DA's Monday description of her statement; the DA said he had not yet obtained the full interview transcript from the university. Cornell confirmed it will bring in outside counsel for an independent probe, after Gov. Kathy Hochul called for a review. The university also edited a public FAQ that had said campus police shared all investigatory information with the DA's office.

    Why impact 3: A reopened criminal review and outside campus probe put university and prosecutor accountability under statewide scrutiny.
