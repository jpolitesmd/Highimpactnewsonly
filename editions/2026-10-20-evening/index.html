  1. Impact 3

    Iran will not hold talks with Washington until its demands are met, its leaders told Pakistan's army chief, a key mediator, in Tehran on Tuesday.

    President Masoud Pezeshkian met Field Marshal Asim Munir to discuss mediation, according to Iranian media. Parliament speaker Mohammad Baqer Qalibaf said the U.S. could not regain Iran's trust until it meets the seven conditions Iran set last month for reopening the Strait of Hormuz, including ending the U.S. blockade of Iran's ports, lifting sanctions on its oil exports and ending hostilities on all regional fronts. Security chief Mohsen Rezaei delivered the same message and said all enriched nuclear material will stay in Iran. Pakistan's earlier mediation helped produce an interim deal in June that unraveled when fighting resumed weeks later. Vice President JD Vance said earlier this month that Iran must make a "meaningful" cut in its enrichment capacity to end the eight-month war.

    Why impact 3: The war's main diplomatic channel is stalled, with both sides holding to their conditions.

  2. Impact 3

    The death toll from Monday's drone strike on a market in Abyei, on the Sudan-South Sudan border, has risen to at least 153, AP reported Tuesday.

    The strike hit Amiet Market and a nearby fuel depot, setting off a large fire; the morning edition reported at least 73 dead. Reuters also cited an official saying more than 150 people were killed. Local officials blamed Sudan's military; that has not been independently confirmed. Abyei is claimed by both Sudan and South Sudan.

    Why impact 3: It is among the deadliest single attacks of Sudan's war.

  3. Impact 3

    Hibsago, a GSK injection for chronic hepatitis B, won FDA approval Tuesday as the first treatment that may let some patients stop all hepatitis B medicine.

    The FDA approved Hibsago (bepirovirsen), given with existing antiviral drugs, for certain adults without cirrhosis who have taken antivirals for at least six months, have suppressed virus levels and have hepatitis B surface antigen of 3,000 IU/mL or less. It is the first antisense drug approved for the disease. In two placebo-controlled trials, 24% of patients on Hibsago qualified to stop their existing medicine, versus none on placebo, and 19% still had the virus controlled 24 weeks after stopping all treatment. It carries a boxed warning for serious liver reactions and low platelet counts. The FDA also authorized a Roche test to pick eligible patients.

    Why impact 3: Most hepatitis B patients now take antiviral pills for life.

  4. Impact 3

    The Fraud Whistleblower Incentive Program, a new $25 million Justice Department fund, will pay tipsters $10,000 to $30,000 when their tips lead to fraud charges.

    The Justice Department's National Fraud Enforcement Division runs the program, and the Treasury Department is committing $25 million to it. Awards are paid at the department's discretion once criminal charges are filed, rather than after a case ends, and tipsters can still qualify for larger awards under other programs. It covers federal health care fraud, with priority on hospice, home health care, day care and special services, and fraud on programs run by the Agriculture Department (such as SNAP), Labor, HHS, HUD, Education, Veterans Affairs, the Department of War and the Small Business Administration. People who took part in the fraud are not eligible. Tips can be submitted now at america.gov/fraud. President Trump announced the program with Vice President JD Vance, who chairs the White House fraud task force.

    Why impact 3: Anyone can now collect a federal cash reward for reporting fraud in health care and benefit programs such as SNAP, unemployment and student aid.

  5. Impact 3

    Online dating services must warn users who got messages from members banned for fraud, under one of 10 bills President Trump signed into law Tuesday.

    The Romance Scam Prevention Act requires the fraud-ban notices. The other laws make Arizona's Chiricahua National Monument a national park; give ministers and certain religious workers two years to ask the IRS to revoke their exemption from Social Security coverage (the Clergy Act); order Labor Department training to spot human trafficking; order a Commerce study on making in the U.S. high-demand products now imported because of supply limits; extend Interior's authority to collect oil and gas permit fees; and transfer or place in trust land in Arizona, Washington, Alaska and Mississippi.

    Why impact 3: New federal rules now apply to dating apps and a new national park is created.

  6. Impact 3

    British fighter jets will fly defensive missions over Saudi Arabia to help shoot down Houthi missiles and drones, Britain's Defence Ministry said Tuesday.

    RAF Typhoon jets will protect Saudi cities and critical infrastructure and will not strike the Houthis, the ministry said. Britain will also send an A400M transport plane and military advisers and extend its air-to-air refueling support for Saudi air operations, including those against Houthi military targets in Yemen. Defence Secretary Wes Streeting cited the Houthis' targeting of King Khalid International Airport in Riyadh. Britain already has air-defense equipment and personnel in the kingdom.

    Why impact 3: Britain is joining the air defense of a Gulf ally as Houthi attacks widen.

  7. Impact 3

    U.S. housing starts fell 5.2% in September, and contracts to buy existing homes dropped to the lowest level on record, two reports showed Tuesday.

    Builders started homes at a 1.221 million annual rate, down from 1.288 million in August and 7.4% below a year earlier, the Census Bureau and HUD said; single-family starts fell 4.6% to 859,000, and building permits slipped 0.2% to 1.40 million. Separately, the National Association of Realtors' pending home sales index fell 7.3% to 66.0, the lowest since the series began in 2001, and was down 11.3% from a year earlier, with declines in all four regions. NAR chief economist Lawrence Yun blamed fast-rising mortgage rates.

    Why impact 3: Higher mortgage rates are freezing both home building and home buying.

  8. Impact 2

    Netflix shares fell about 5% after hours Tuesday after its fourth-quarter revenue forecast came in below analysts' estimates.

    Netflix reported third-quarter revenue of $12.87 billion, up about 12% and in line with its own forecast, and earnings of 87 cents a share. It forecast fourth-quarter revenue of $13.44 billion, below the roughly $13.51 billion analysts expected.

    Why impact 2: Netflix is the first big streaming company to report this quarter.

    MoneyAP ↗
  9. Impact 2

    SpaceX's revenue rose 161% to $14.6 billion in the third quarter and it earned $4.4 billion, the company reported Tuesday.

    A year earlier SpaceX lost $1.1 billion. Its artificial-intelligence segment brought in $7.6 billion, up from $813 million, helped by cloud-services deals and its August purchase of Cursor; Starlink and other connectivity revenue rose 69% to $5.2 billion, with 13.6 million Starlink consumer subscribers. Capital spending reached $48.1 billion in the first nine months. It was SpaceX's second quarterly report since its June listing on Nasdaq.

    Why impact 2: The newly public company's results show its spending on AI is now driving most of its revenue.

    Kenya's Ebola cases rose to nine after a family contact of the country's first patient tested positive while in quarantine, health officials said.

    All cases are in one cluster, with no sign of spread beyond it, the Health Ministry said; one patient has died and the other eight are stable. Officials said 134 of 135 known contacts have been traced. Kenya has not imposed travel, trade or gathering restrictions.
